Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Digital Goods Delivery

The "Golden Membership" is a digital upgrade. Upon a successful payment, the Golden Star badge and priority ranking are applied to your account automatically and immediately.

2. Refund Eligibility

Because the Golden Membership is an immediate digital good, all sales are final and non-refundable once the badge is successfully applied to your account.

3. Payment Failures & Errors

If your money is deducted from your bank account but the Golden Membership is not activated due to a technical error, please contact our support team. In such verified failure cases, the transaction will be refunded to your original method of payment within 5-7 working days.

4. Cancellations

As this is a one-time digital purchase (not a subscription), there is no recurring cancellation required.
